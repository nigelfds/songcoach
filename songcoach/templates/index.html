Apple Music mode

Play a song or playlist in Apple Music — SongCoach captures each song, pausing with the music, and queues every finished song for stems. It keeps going until you hit Stop.

SongCoach needs permission to read Apple Music. Grant it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation, then try again.
