How this works

Your recording is split into stems. Blend DRUMS and NO DRUMS with the faders and switch each one in or out of the mix — or tap REF to hear the untouched full song. Drag across any waveform to mark an A–B section, then LOOP it and ease off the SPEED to drill the tricky bits.

Keyboard
Space
Play / pause
I / O
Set section in / out (A / B) at the playhead
L
Toggle loop
← / →
Seek 5 seconds
⇧ ← / →
Nudge B (out) by 0.1s
⌥ ← / →
Nudge A (in) by 0.1s
Backspace
